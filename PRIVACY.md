# Privacy policy

_Workspace and Workspace Calendar, desktop apps. Last updated: 26 November 2026._

**In short: your data stays on your computer, and on servers you choose to connect. Nothing is
sent to us.**

## What the apps keep, and where

- **Your work** (pages, databases, files, calendars and events) is stored on your computer, in
  the app's data folder. If you connect a Workspace server, it syncs with that server, which you
  or your organization run.
- **Sign-ins** (to a Workspace server, Google, a CalDAV server) are stored on your computer,
  encrypted with your system's keyring where there is one.
- **Logs** (Workspace Calendar) are on your computer. They never contain tokens or the contents
  of your events.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.** The apps don't send usage data anywhere.
- **Updates:** the apps download update information and new versions from the project's GitHub
  releases. GitHub sees your IP address when they do; nothing else is sent.

## Google user data

If you choose to connect a Google account:

- **Workspace Calendar** asks to see your list of calendars and to read and change their
  events. It shows them, and sends the changes you make. It keeps a copy of the events you chose
  on your computer, so it works offline.
- **Workspace** asks for `drive.file`: it can see only the Drive files you pick with Google's
  Picker. It keeps their name, kind, size and dates on your computer, to show them in your pages.
- Data from Google goes only between your computer and Google. It isn't sent to the developers
  or to anyone else, isn't used for advertising, and isn't used to train any model.
- You can disconnect at any time: **Disconnect** (Workspace, Settings → Connections) or remove
  the account (Workspace Calendar, its ⋯ menu). That removes the sign-in and the copied data
  from your computer. You can also remove the apps' access in your
  [Google account](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.
